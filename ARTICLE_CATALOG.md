# Article Catalog — Precast.Concrete.co.id

## Editorial contract

This catalog expands every parent topic in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md` into six non-geographic, non-duplicative briefs. Commercial product hubs keep product-overview, availability, and quotation intent. Articles answer narrower informational intents and must link back to the relevant hub without copying its sales copy.

Every article must:

- identify the owner, designer, precaster, laboratory, transporter, rigging/crane provider, erector, contractor, supervisor, and regulator roles when relevant;
- use current primary standards, project specifications, verified product documents, and qualified reviewers;
- label assumptions, examples, and illustrative diagrams;
- avoid inventing dimensions, capacities, loads, spans, reinforcement, cover, mix proportions, lifting data, tolerances, fire ratings, price, stock, lead time, certification, service area, or company capability;
- stop short of project-specific structural, hydraulic, geotechnical, lifting, transport, temporary-works, erection, fire, or repair design;
- contain no location-swapped title, slug, or city/province article variant.

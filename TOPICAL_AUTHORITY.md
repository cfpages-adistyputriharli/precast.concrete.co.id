# Topical Authority Map — Precast.Concrete.co.id

## Purpose and positioning

This map turns Precast.Concrete.co.id into a non-geographic Indonesian knowledge hub about precast concrete systems. It covers how products are selected, specified, produced, inspected, transported, erected, procured, and maintained without presenting generic content as project-specific engineering advice.

The safest current positioning is **independent precast information, quotation, supplier/contractor referral, and project-facilitation platform**. The repository does not, by itself, prove that the site owner is a legal manufacturer, designer, testing laboratory, transporter, erector, or standards-certification body. Those roles may only be claimed after documented verification.

## Universal evidence gates

These gates apply to every topic and article:

1. **Standards and regulation:** cite the current official Indonesian standard, project specification, authority guidance, or other primary source. Verify edition, scope, and applicability before naming a clause or value.
2. **Engineering:** loads, spans, reinforcement, prestress, concrete cover, connections, lifting anchors, temporary bracing, foundations, fire resistance, tolerances, and repair acceptance require a competent project engineer. Articles explain inputs and review processes, not final designs.
3. **Products:** dimensions, mass, strength class, exposure class, capacity, test data, certificates, stock, price, lead time, warranty, and service area must come from a dated supplier/manufacturer document for the exact unit.
4. **Operations:** lifting plans, transport routes, crane selection, rigging, erection sequences, exclusion zones, and temporary works must be prepared and approved by qualified parties for the actual site.
5. **Commercial claims:** company identity, factory ownership, years in business, project counts, “best/#1,” national reach, and direct-manufacturer status need auditable business records and must identify the party making the claim.
6. **Images and case studies:** use owned/licensed media, name the project and role only with permission, and distinguish illustrative examples from verified performance evidence.
7. **Safety:** content must tell readers when to stop work, isolate a suspect unit, and escalate to qualified personnel; it must not normalize field improvisation.

## Roles that must remain explicit

The owner, designer/engineer, precaster/manufacturer, materials supplier, independent testing laboratory, transport provider, crane/rigging contractor, erector, main contractor, site supervisor, inspector, maintenance provider, and regulator can have different responsibilities. Articles must identify who supplies information, who reviews it, who approves it, and who executes the work rather than collapsing all roles into “supplier.”

## Recommended information architecture

- `/beton-precast/` — primary commercial/category hub.
- Existing product routes — product-specific commercial hubs.
- `/panduan/` — fundamentals, selection, design inputs, procurement, and lifecycle guides.
- `/produksi/` — materials, reinforcement, moulds, casting, curing, and QA.
- `/instalasi/` — logistics, lifting, site readiness, erection, joints, and safety.
- `/perawatan/` — durability, inspection, diagnosis, repair, and replacement.

The path labels are recommendations, not an instruction to move existing URLs without redirect and traffic analysis.

## Publication sequence

1. Correct the site's role and unsupported claims; settle canonical product hubs.
2. Publish P0 safety, evidence, procurement, logistics, erection, and QA foundations.
3. Add product-system decision clusters linked bidirectionally with their commercial hubs.
4. Add production, durability, and maintenance depth after qualified review and original evidence are available.
5. Expand only when Search Console queries, sales questions, inspection findings, or documented projects reveal a distinct unmet intent.
